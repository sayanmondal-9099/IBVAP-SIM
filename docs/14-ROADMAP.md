# 14 - Roadmap

## Purpose
Outline the high-level development phases and milestones for IBVAP-SIM.

## Scope
From initial repository scaffolding to final SIH demonstration delivery.

## Terminology
- **Phase**: A distinct period of development focused on specific, measurable deliverables.

## Requirements
- **MGT-RMP-001**: The roadmap must maintain a clear separation of concerns, executing infrastructure setup, simulation logic, and UI development in logical sequence.

## Dependencies
- Development resource availability and time constraints.

## Assumptions
- The SIH hackathon or evaluation deadline acts as the hard final target date.

## Open Decisions
- OPEN ARCHITECTURAL DECISION: Prioritization of advanced radar simulation versus multi-camera tracking simulation based on time constraints.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Roadmap milestones are actively tracked and updated as development progresses through the phases.
