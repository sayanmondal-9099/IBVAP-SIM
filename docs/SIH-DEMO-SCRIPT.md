# SIH Demo Script: IBVAP-SIM

**Estimated Duration**: 7-10 minutes
**Target Audience**: Smart India Hackathon Judges
**Primary Focus**: Simulation architecture, data flow, rule engine reliability, and auditability.

---

## 1. Introduction (1 min)
*Start on the Command Map view (http://localhost:5173)*

**Speaker:**
"Welcome to the IBVAP-SIM demonstration. IBVAP stands for Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform. What you are seeing today is our **Simulation-Only Prototype**. 
We have strictly isolated this prototype from any real defence networks, cameras, or databases. Everything on screen—every drone, every sensor, every alert—is synthetically generated by our edge simulation engine to prove our data-flow and rule architecture works reliably at scale."

## 2. The Golden Demo: Drone in Restricted Airspace (3 mins)
*Navigate to 'Simulation' tab. Select 'Drone enters restricted airspace' and click Start.*

**Speaker:**
"Our edge simulation engine is now generating a synthetic scenario. Here, a drone is moving into a restricted zone.
Notice the top indicator confirming 'SIMULATION ONLY'.

*Navigate to '3D View'.*
"In our 3D Operational View, we can visualize the incoming telemetry. The backend fuses simulated camera and radar data in real-time."

*Navigate to 'Alerts' tab.*
"As the drone crosses the virtual tripwire, our rules engine on the backend detects the breach and deduplicates the events (using a 60-second cooldown window). It generates a high-priority Alert (P1 or P2). The system calculates a confidence and quality score based on the simulated sensors."

*Click 'Acknowledge' on the alert.*
"The operator acknowledges the alert to signal it is being reviewed."

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop & Incident Creation (2 mins)
*Navigate to 'Human Review' tab.*

**Speaker:**
"We strongly believe in human-in-the-loop validation. An operator reviews the evidence—timestamp, location, and confidence score. Once validated, they promote this Alert into a formal Incident."

*Promote the alert to an incident.*
*Navigate to 'Incidents' tab.*

"The incident is now tracked. Once the response team completes their simulated action, we resolve the incident on the dashboard."
*Click Resolve.*

## 4. Cryptographic Audit Trail (2 mins)
*Navigate to 'Audit' tab.*

**Speaker:**
"Every critical action—from the edge simulation generating the alert, to the operator acknowledging it, to incident resolution—is recorded in a cryptographically chained audit log. 
If anyone attempts to tamper with the database to hide an incident, the SHA-256 chain will break. This guarantees append-only compliance for border security operations."

## 5. Resilience Demonstration: Sensor Degradation (2 mins)
*Navigate to 'Simulation' tab. Select 'Camera degraded/obstructed' and click Start.*
*Navigate to 'Health' tab.*

**Speaker:**
"To prove the resilience of our architecture, we can simulate hardware failures. In this scenario, a camera's quality degrades due to weather or obstruction.
Our dashboard instantly reflects this degradation. The rules engine adjusts the confidence score of incoming tracks dynamically, preventing false positives while relying on the secondary radar sensor to maintain tracking."

## 6. Conclusion
**Speaker:**
"In summary, IBVAP-SIM demonstrates a complete, end-to-end edge-to-cloud architecture. We have proven real-time tracking, rule-based alerts, human-in-the-loop workflows, cryptographic auditability, and offline resilience—all within a strict, safe, simulation-only boundary. Thank you."
